Judge the year by the quality of your decisions, the consistency of your risk controls, your understanding of what you own, and your honesty about the evidence. A profitable trade that broke your rules can teach a dangerous habit, and a planned loss can be the right result of a disciplined decision. The goal is not to trade more. It is to decide better and protect what you have.
